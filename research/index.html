Research

Research projects

  • 2025–present

    Vertical mixing of surface ozone

    Reproducing the daily cycle of surface ozone in Yongin, Korea, with a one-dimensional multilayer column model, and quantifying how much the ozone stored overnight in the residual layer adds as it is mixed down in the morning.

  • 2019–present

    Southern Hemisphere westerlies in the Last Glacial Maximum

    Showing with CESM experiments how expanded Antarctic sea ice strengthened the Southern Hemisphere westerlies and shifted them poleward during the Last Glacial Maximum.

Contract projects

  • 2025–present

    Regional contributions to particulate matter in central Korea

    Calculating how much each region of central Korea contributes to particulate matter with the CAMx air-quality model, which tags pollution by the region it came from (PSAT).

Personal projects

  • 2026

    AI-based icing risk on Jeonbuk expressways

    Estimating road-surface temperature and icing every ten minutes where three Jeonbuk expressways have no road-weather station, and mapping icing risk for every 500 m section — an entry to the 2026 DATA·AI Analysis Competition (KISTI).
